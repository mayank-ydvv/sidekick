Layout: menu + toolbar on top (transport, tempo, pattern selector, view buttons), Browser on the left, Channel Rack (F6), Piano Roll (F7), Playlist (F5), Mixer (F9).
Workflow: add instruments in the Channel Rack ("+" at the bottom), program steps by clicking step buttons, open a channel's Piano Roll by right-clicking it > Piano roll, paint notes with the pencil, arrange patterns in the Playlist with the paint tool.
Transport: play/stop Space, pattern vs song mode toggle next to play (L), record R. Route a channel to a mixer insert with the "Insert" number box in the channel settings. Export: File > Export (⌘R) > WAV/MP3.
