Layout: menu bar on top, options bar under it (settings for the current tool), toolbar on the left, panels on the right (Layers, Properties, Adjustments, History), canvas in the middle; contextual task bar floats below selections.
Tools: move V, marquee M, lasso L, quick/object selection W, crop C, brush B, eraser E, clone S, healing J, text T, gradient/fill G, zoom Z, hand Space.
Tasks: new layer ⇧⌘N, adjustment layer via the half-circle icon at the bottom of Layers, free transform ⌘T, deselect ⌘D, invert selection ⇧⌘I, generative fill from the contextual task bar after a selection, export File > Export > Export As (⌥⇧⌘W).
