Layout: toolbar on top (View, Zoom, Add Slide, Play, Table, Chart, Text, Shape, Media, Comment, Collaborate), slide navigator on the left, canvas in the middle, inspector on the right with Format / Animate / Document tabs.
Tasks: new slide ⇧⌘N or the "+" Add Slide button, change layout in Format > Slide Layout (with nothing selected), build in/out animations in the Animate tab, slide transitions with a slide selected in Animate, presenter notes View > Show Presenter Notes, play ⌥⌘P.
Export: File > Export To > PowerPoint/PDF/Movie.
