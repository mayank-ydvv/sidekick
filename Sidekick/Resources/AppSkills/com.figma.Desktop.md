Shortcuts: move V, frame F (or A), rectangle R, ellipse O, line L, pen P, text T, hand Space-drag, zoom to fit ⇧1, zoom to selection ⇧2, group ⌘G, frame selection ⌥⌘G, component ⌥⌘K, auto layout ⇧A, duplicate ⌘D, show/hide UI ⌘\, quick actions ⌘/.
Export: select layer > right panel bottom "Export" > +. Share: Share button top right. Prototype: switch right panel to Prototype and drag connection noodles.
