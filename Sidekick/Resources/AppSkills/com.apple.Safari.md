Layout: tab bar + combined address/search field at top; sidebar toggle (left of tabs) shows bookmarks, reading list, tab groups; Share button and "+" new tab at top right.
Shortcuts: new tab ⌘T, close ⌘W, reopen closed tab ⇧⌘T, focus address bar ⌘L, find on page ⌘F, reader mode ⇧⌘R, bookmarks ⌥⌘B, history ⌘Y, downloads ⌥⌘L, zoom ⌘+/⌘−, private window ⇧⌘N.
Settings: Safari > Settings (⌘,) — tabs for General, Tabs, AutoFill, Passwords, Search, Security, Privacy, Websites, Extensions, Advanced (enable Develop menu there).
Clear history: History > Clear History. Extensions: Settings > Extensions.
